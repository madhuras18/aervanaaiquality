#  AERVANA
### The Air We Breathe. The Future We Shape.

**AERVANA** is an AI-powered Urban Air Quality Assistant designed to help individuals and families understand air pollution, make informed daily decisions, and adopt healthier and more sustainable lifestyles.

It brings air quality awareness, personalized recommendations, indoor air care, and environmental impact tracking together in one platform.

##  About the Project

Air pollution is a growing environmental challenge affecting communities around the world. People often struggle to understand air quality information and translate it into practical everyday actions.

AERVANA aims to bridge this gap by presenting air quality information in an accessible way and demonstrating how small lifestyle changes can contribute to a cleaner environment.

##  Key Features

### 1. Air Quality Intelligence
- Explore air quality information for different cities.
- View AQI and pollutant readings.
- Explore an interactive pollution map.
- Understand different air pollutants and their effects.

### 2. Smart Living
- Personalized daily routine suggestions.
- Outdoor activity recommendations.
- Sustainable transportation alternatives.
- EcoRoute for exploring cleaner travel choices.

### 3. Family Care
- Air quality guidance for families.
- Indoor air quality improvement tips.
- Practical precautions for children and older adults.
- Everyday pollution protection recommendations.

### 4. My Impact
- Track sustainable lifestyle actions.
- Monitor personal environmental contributions.
- View estimated emissions savings.
- Explore weekly progress.

### 5. AI Assistant
- Conversational air quality guidance.
- Answers to pollution-related questions.
- Practical indoor air care suggestions.
- Recommendations for healthier and more sustainable routines.

##  Our Goal

To make air quality awareness more accessible and help people turn environmental information into meaningful everyday actions.

##  Technology

##  Demo Data Disclaimer

AERVANA is currently a demonstration project.

Air quality readings, pollution map values, and environmental impact figures are illustrative sample data and estimates. They should not be interpreted as actual real-time measurements or verified environmental outcomes.

##  Run Locally

Prerequisites: Node.js and npm.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/madhuras18/aervanaaiquality.git

cd aervanaaiquality

npm install

npm run dev
```

Open the local development URL shown in your terminal.
